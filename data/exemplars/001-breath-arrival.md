You're here. You can hear my voice, and your eyes are closed, and for the next little while there's nowhere you have to be.

Notice that you're already breathing. You didn't have to start it. It's been happening this whole time, without you.

Just follow the next breath in. Not deeper than usual. Only notice it.

And the way it leaves.

There's a small pause at the bottom of the breath, after the air goes out, before the next one comes. See if you can find that pause.

You don't have to fill it.

Somewhere in the room there's a sound — the room's own quiet, a hum, something far off. Let it be there. It doesn't need anything from you.

Your shoulders are probably holding a little. They usually are. Let them be heavy. Let the chair take them.

The breath keeps arriving on its own. You're just here to keep it company.

If your mind has wandered off — it has, that's what minds do — there's no failure in it. The breath is still here. Come back to it the way you'd come back to a window.

A little more weight into the chair now. A little less of you holding yourself up.

There's nothing to get right. You're already doing the only thing this asks: you're here, and you're breathing, and you're letting that be enough.

Stay with one more breath.

And when you're ready — no rush — let your eyes open, and bring this quiet with you into whatever's next.
