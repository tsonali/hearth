You're lying down, and your eyes are closed, and you don't have to stay awake for this. If you drift off before I finish, that's the whole point. You can't do it wrong.

Your brain is still going. Of course it is. We're not going to argue with it. We're just going to give it something slower to do.

Feel the weight of your body on the bed. Not holding yourself anywhere. The bed has you.

Start at your feet. Just notice they're there, and let them be heavy. That's all. Heavy, and done for the day.

Let the heaviness move up — the legs now, giving their weight to the bed. Nothing to hold up anymore.

The thoughts are still coming. Let them come. You don't have to follow them. Let them pass like cars you're not in.

The belly, rising and falling on its own. You don't have to breathe on purpose. It's been handling it all day.

Your hands. Let them be heavy. Each finger a little heavier than you were keeping it.

The jaw — let it come unclenched. The space behind the eyes — let it go dark and soft.

Whatever you didn't finish today will still be there tomorrow. It doesn't need you tonight. You can put it down.

Heavier now. Slower. The bed holding all of you.

You don't have to listen to the rest of this. You can let my voice get further away.

Heavier. Slower. Down.
