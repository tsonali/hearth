You're here, eyes closed, and you've been carrying something today. We're not going to solve it. We're just going to set it down for a few minutes.

First, notice you're holding it. There's a place in the body where the day's weight has gathered. Find it without fixing it.

You don't have to let go of the thing itself yet. Just loosen your grip on it by a little. The way you'd ease a hand that's been clenched too long.

It's still there. That's allowed. You're not pretending it's gone. You're just not gripping it so hard.

Notice your breath has been shallow, the way it goes when we brace. Let one breath go lower. Then let it leave all the way.

Whatever it is — it doesn't need you to hold it every second. It will still be there when you pick it back up. You're allowed to put it down for now.

Feel what it's like to not be carrying it for this one minute. The arms a little lighter. The chest a little wider.

If it creeps back into your hands, that's fine. Just set it down again. You can do that as many times as you need.

There's nothing you have to resolve right now. The only task is rest, and you're doing it.

Stay here, lighter, a moment more.

When you're ready, let your eyes open. The thing will be there — but you'll pick it up with a little more room than you had.
