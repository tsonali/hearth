# The pitch / manifesto — DRAFT in Sonali's voice (for her sign-off)

This is a first pass at the public-facing pitch, drafted from Sonali's own framing
(2026-05-30). Her voice, her call — Claude drafted it to get the terminology sharp;
Sonali edits/owns the final. NOT final copy.

---

## The opening (the hook)

> Are you tired of selling your soul *and* your life savings to the tech oligarchs —
> the ones who want to own both your data and your money?

## Who's saying it (the credentials, worn lightly)

> I'm a recovering lawyer and a Stanford lecturer. I'm **anti-tokenmaxxing**
> (you should never pay rent, by the token, forever, to think a private thought).
> I'm **pro-privacy** (what happens on your machine is nobody's business but yours).
> I'm **public domain, not "open source"** — no attribution, no copyleft, none of
> the indecipherable license nonsense; just *take it, it's yours.* I think
> **functional code shouldn't be copyrightable at all**, and I'm done waiting for
> the law to catch up — so this is that argument, made real.

## The mission

> So I set out to do a simple, radical thing: **legally give you private, local AI
> that runs on your own computer, works as well as it possibly can, and costs you
> nothing — ever.** No cloud. No account. No tokens. No telemetry. Nothing leaves
> your device. Not because I promise it won't — because it *can't*. You can read
> every line. You can turn off your wifi and watch it keep working.

## The products (the suite — what you actually get)

> Here they are. Free. Yours.
>
> 1. **A companion that refuses to fool you.** A chatbot built to do the opposite of
>    every other one — it actively *limits* how much you anthropomorphize it. It
>    won't pretend to be your friend, won't fake a soul it doesn't have. It's an
>    honest mirror that helps you hear yourself. (The thing Weizenbaum wished ELIZA
>    had been.)
> 2. **An imagination engine in your own voice.** Tell it what you want to imagine;
>    it writes you a guided session and reads it to you — *in your own voice* —
>    while you close your eyes and do the imagining yourself.
> 3. *(… the suite grows: reflective journaling, rehearsal, make-sense-of-your-own-
>    stuff, and more — each a focused private instrument, never an open everything-box.)*

## The kicker (the deeper offer)

> And because none of it should be locked up: **a free consumer product that lets
> people do this themselves.** Bring your own data. Make your own companion — a
> character you love, a voice that soothes you, a mirror that fits you. You don't
> need me. You don't need them. You just need your own machine.

---

## Terminology notes (so the language stays sharp)
- **tokenmaxxing** = the metered, pay-per-token, rent-forever cloud model. We're against it.
- **public domain, not "open source"** = the precise distinction. OSS still imposes
  license obligations (attribution, copyleft, propagation). CC0/public-domain imposes none.
- **anti-code-copyright** = the belief functional code shouldn't be copyrightable; the
  CC0 release is the thesis embodied.
- **honest companion / anti-anthropomorphism** = Sonali's personal/product stance
  (Family C), distinct from the framework itself (which is value-neutral tech).
