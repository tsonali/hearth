# Protocol: Future-Self Visualization

This document defines what a good future-self visualization session contains. It is the **source of truth** for session quality. The script generator (Task 03) personalizes *within* this structure; the structure itself is not optional.

This is the founder's domain. The scaffold below is a starting point drawn from the established structure of guided-imagery and mental-rehearsal practice — the founder should revise it to match her own judgment and the thinking in *Unreality*.

---

## What this protocol is

A guided session in which the user vividly imagines a specific, desired future version of themselves — having achieved a goal, or moved through a situation well — in order to clarify intention, build motivation, and rehearse a desired internal state. It is non-clinical: the worst case of a mediocre session is "that was flat," not harm. That is why it is the v0 template.

## Session structure

A session has three parts. All three must always be present. The return is non-negotiable — the session must always bring the user back.

### 1. Opening / settle (induction)
- Orient the user: eyes closed, comfortable, this is their time.
- Slow the user down: a few breaths, a brief body settle, releasing the immediate day.
- Lower the pace and lower the stakes. No goal yet — just arrival.

### 2. Visualization body
- Move the user to a future point in time — the horizon gathered in intake.
- Build the future self concretely and sensorily: where they are, what they see, how they carry themselves, how it *feels in the body* to be this version of themselves.
- Anchor on the specifics from intake — the user's own details, not generic ones.
- Let the future self "look back" — how did they get here, what did they do, what did the present-day user need to know. This converts the visualization from fantasy into something motivational and directional.
- Stay in present tense, second person. The user *is* there, not imagining being there.

### 3. Return / re-orientation
- Gently bring the user back from the future to the present moment.
- Carry something back: a feeling, an image, a piece of knowing — a bridge from the session into the real day.
- Re-orient to the room: the body, the breath, the space, eyes opening when ready.
- The user should end grounded, present, and clear — never left adrift.

## Voice and pacing
- Second person, present tense throughout.
- Calm, warm, unhurried. Spacious.
- Generous pauses, especially after evocative or sensory prompts — the user needs real time to render the image.
- Invitational language ("you might notice...", "perhaps...") rather than commanding.

## Personalization — what intake must gather
For the generator to produce a good session, intake (Task 02) should surface:
- The goal or desired future state, in the user's own words
- The time horizon (when is this future self — months, a year, five years)
- The current obstacle or what they're spiraling on
- The desired *feeling* — the internal state they want to reach
- Concrete, sensory, personal details to anchor the visualization (places, people, specifics)

## Quality bar
A good session: correctly structured, genuinely personalized to the user's specifics, well-paced for spoken delivery, and always ending grounded. The founder is the quality judge. If a generated session is generic, rushed, or weak on the return, it is not done.

## Out of scope for this protocol
Anything trauma-, grief-, or exposure-adjacent. Those are different protocols with different safety requirements and are not part of v0.
