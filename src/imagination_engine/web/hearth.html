First time here? Everything runs on this computer — nothing you type or hear is uploaded. The very first session takes about a minute to wake the model up (one time); after that it's quick. Pick a tool below to begin.

Hearth

Private AI that lives
in your house.

A small set of focused tools, each running entirely on this machine. Nothing you type is uploaded, metered, or sent anywhere — not because we promise it won't, but because it cannot. Everything here is in the public domain.

The Tools

Imagination Engine

Tell it what you want to imagine; it writes a guided session and reads it back in your own voice.

The Secretary

Draft and reply to messages, summarize, rewrite, pull out the to-dos. Everyday writing work on your own words.

Companion

An honest mirror that reflects your thinking back to you. Not a friend — a surface to think against.

Ask Your Files

Point it at your own notes and documents and ask. It answers from what is there, or says it does not know.

Build Your Own

Describe an instrument, point it at your data, and keep it. Yours, on your machine.
