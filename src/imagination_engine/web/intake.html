Tell me what you want to imagine. The world is open. You could:

  • visualize yourself meeting a hard-fought goal
  • imagine being a different character — someone braver, calmer, more curious
  • imagine your life if you'd taken a different path
  • imagine being Abraham Lincoln the night before Gettysburg
  • imagine being Taylor Swift backstage before a show

Or anything else.

For real living people, the engine works best on the felt experience of being them — the body, the room, the moment — not on getting their latest news right. If a current fact matters to your imagining (the tour they're on, who they're with, what just happened), type it into your prompt and the engine will work with it.
