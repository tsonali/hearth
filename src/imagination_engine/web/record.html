Recording tips
  • Read in your natural, calm voice — not a "guide voice." The model will learn whatever you give it.
  • Sit a hand's distance from the microphone. Stay at roughly the same distance throughout.
  • If you stumble on a sentence, redo it. The model is faithful to its inputs — a clean dataset beats a long messy one.
  • Take breaks every 15–20 minutes. Voice quality drifts when you're tired and the model will hear it.
  • If a sentence feels wrong for your voice (a phrase you'd never say), skip it. Skipped sentences can be returned to later.
