Welcome.

This is a tool for guided imagination. Tell me what you want to imagine — succeeding at something hard, being a different character, your life on another path, being a real or fictional person. I'll write you a ten-to-fifteen minute session and read it back. You listen with your eyes closed and do the imagining yourself.

Your own voice · optional

Or have the engine read in your voice.

By default the engine offers her (a warm woman) or him (a slow measured man) — two curated voices that sound like audiobook narrators. If you'd rather hear yourself read your own sessions back, you can train your own voice: about thirty minutes of recording, then a longer training step that runs overnight on this machine. After that, "your own voice" becomes a third option at intake time.

About

What you should know before starting.

// How it works

A small language model running on this machine has a short conversation with you (the intake). It hands what you said to a longer model call that writes a 2,500-word session. A neural text-to-speech model then reads it aloud, paragraph by paragraph, with real pauses between thoughts. You listen.

// Privacy

No part of this app reaches the internet during use. Model weights live in ~/.cache/imagination_engine. The session database lives in data/memory.sqlite. If you record your own voice, the recordings live in data/recordings/. None of it is transmitted anywhere. You own the file; deleting it deletes the history.

// What this isn't

Not a therapy app. Not a companion. There is no thread to scroll back through, no streaks, no notifications, no nudges to come back. It's an instrument you open to do focused imagination work and then close to go live your day.
