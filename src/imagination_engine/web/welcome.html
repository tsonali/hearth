ABOUT THIS

What it is

A guided imagination tool you run on your own laptop. You bring an imagining; it writes a session around what you brought and reads it to you. The protocol shape is the same every time — a settle, then the imagining, then a gentle return. The content is yours.

How it works

A small language model running on this machine has a short conversation with you (the "intake"). It hands what you said to a longer model call that writes a 2,500-word session. A neural text-to-speech model then reads it aloud, paragraph by paragraph, with real pauses between thoughts. You listen.

Privacy

No part of this app reaches the internet during use. The model weights live in a cache directory; the database lives in data/memory.sqlite. If you record your own voice the recordings live in data/recordings/. None of it is transmitted anywhere. You own the file; deleting it deletes the history.

What this isn't

Not a therapy app. Not a companion. There is no thread to scroll back through, no streaks, no nudges to come back. It's an instrument you open to do focused imagination work and then close to go live your day.
